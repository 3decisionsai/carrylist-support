# Carrylist Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026.

Carrylist does not collect, transmit, sell or track personal data. Trip names, traveler names, packing items, notes and progress stay locally on your device. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, backends or AI services.

Sharing a checklist or exporting a backup sends the content through the destination you select. That destination's terms apply. Your Apple device backup settings may include app data in device backups. Carrylist does not operate cloud storage.

Delete trips within the app or delete the app to remove its local data. Export a backup first if you want a copy. Local recovery copies may remain in the app container to recover damaged saves; deleting the app removes those copies.

Carrylist does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo-library access.
